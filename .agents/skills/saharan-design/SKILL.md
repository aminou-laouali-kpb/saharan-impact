# Saharan Impact Design System & Copy Rules

## Design System "L'aube au Sahel"

### Color Tokens
- `--nuit-sahara`: `#10141C` (Deep desert night, primary background, immersive sections)
- `--pierre-froide`: `#ECEDE8` (Cold stone, background for light/contrasting sections)
- `--or-de-l-aube`: `#D8A24A` (Dawn gold, accent warm color, horizon glow)
- `--bleu-horizon`: `#2E6070` (Horizon blue, accent secondary cold color - Dubai side)
- `--terre`: `#191D26` (Terre / Ground, text color on light backgrounds)
- `--sable-attenue`: `#B7B2A6` (Attenuated sand, secondary text / legend on dark backgrounds)

### Typography
- **Display**: Bricolage Grotesque (700/800). Reserved for action-oriented headers.
- **Body**: Geist (400/500). Clean, readable, neutral.
- **Data / Code / Monospace**: Geist Mono. For computing states, Diagnostic Éclair output.

### The Signature "Ligne d'Horizon"
A golden horizontal line crossing the page:
- In the hero, it forms the frontier between the indigo night (top) and dawn gold glow (bottom).
- The *Diagnostic Éclair* element rises from this line.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for scroll animations.

## Copywriting Guidelines

### Hero
- Eyebrow: `Sahel · Implémentation IA & solutions numériques`
- Titre: **« L'équipement est arrivé. L'usage, non. »**
- Sous-titre: *« Saharan Impact transforme les investissements numériques des institutions sahéliennes en résultats mesurables — par l'IA appliquée, l'automatisation des flux et des plateformes conçues pour le terrain. »*
- CTA démo: `Décrivez un processus qui vous coûte du temps →`

### Sections & Narrative
1. **Situation / Complication**: Explain why millions of digital investments in the Sahel remain unused in server rooms.
2. **Résolution (Offre)**: Convert existing infrastructure into capacity: Applied AI, flow automation, and team training.
3. **Preuves**: Showcase real solutions: national NGO registry, employment monitoring (GWANI/PEEJ), RNCL platform, n8n automations.
4. **Méthode**: Short, verifiable milestones.
5. **Pont Niamey-Dubaï**: Highlight the unique Niamey/Dubai position.
